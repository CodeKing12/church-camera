Church Camera

Point the phone at the stage, then start.

Sideways is better here

In mirror mode the whole screen is what goes out, so holding the phone upright broadcasts an upright picture with the stage squeezed into a strip. Turn the phone sideways — and turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Centre if it will not rotate.

Add to Home Screen first

Running inside Safari puts the address bar and the toolbar into the picture, and Safari can suspend the camera when it feels like it. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Church Camera from the home screen.
